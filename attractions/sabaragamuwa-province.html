Discover the gem-rich heart of Sri Lanka, from the sacred heights of Sri Pada and the rainforest landscapes of Ratnapura to the elephant experiences, heritage sites and scenic mountain valleys of Kegalle.

The Land of Gems, Sacred Mountains and Nature

Sabaragamuwa Province brings together sacred mountains, ancient religious heritage, traditional gem culture, tropical forests, waterfalls and memorable wildlife experiences. The province is home to Ratnapura and Kegalle, offering travellers a remarkable combination of culture, adventure, nature and history.

Ratnapura District

The famous gem-producing heart of Sri Lanka, surrounded by sacred mountains, forests, waterfalls and rich cultural heritage.

01
🏔️ Sacred Mountain

Sri Pada (Adam's Peak)

One of Sri Lanka's most sacred pilgrimage destinations, famous for its challenging climb, spectacular sunrise views and the sacred footprint at the summit.

📍 Sri Pada, Ratnapura District
