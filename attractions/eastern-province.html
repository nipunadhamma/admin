Explore Eastern Province

Discover the breathtaking eastern coast of Sri Lanka, from the historic harbour city of Trincomalee to the peaceful beaches of Batticaloa and Pasikudah, and the world-famous surfing and wildlife experiences around Arugam Bay.

The Tropical East Coast

Eastern Province offers a remarkable combination of pristine beaches, historic harbour cities, ancient religious landmarks, marine experiences, lagoons, wildlife and world-class surfing. From the cultural heritage of Trincomalee and Batticaloa to the adventure atmosphere of Arugam Bay, the Eastern Province provides unforgettable experiences for every traveller.
