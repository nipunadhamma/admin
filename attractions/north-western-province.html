Explore North Western Province

Discover the North Western Province of Sri Lanka, where ancient royal capitals, dramatic rock fortresses, sacred temples, wildlife and beautiful coastal landscapes come together. Explore the historic cities of Kurunegala and Puttalam, and experience the unique natural beauty of the Wayamba region.

Where Ancient Kingdoms Meet the Coast

North Western Province is one of Sri Lanka's most diverse travel regions. The province combines the ancient royal heritage of Kurunegala with the historic rock fortress of Yapahuwa, archaeological sites, sacred temples, coconut plantations, wildlife landscapes and the spectacular coastal environment of Puttalam and Kalpitiya.

Kurunegala District

A historic heartland of Sri Lanka, surrounded by dramatic rock formations, ancient royal sites, Buddhist temples and beautiful inland landscapes.
