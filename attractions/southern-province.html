The Tropical Soul of Sri Lanka

Southern Province is one of Sri Lanka's most popular travel regions, bringing together centuries of colonial history, ancient Buddhist heritage, tropical beaches, surfing destinations, marine experiences, wetlands and some of the country's most remarkable wildlife landscapes. From the historic Galle Fort to the beaches of Unawatuna, Mirissa and Weligama, and from the cultural heritage of Matara to the wildlife regions around Hambantota, the Southern Province offers an extraordinary variety of experiences for every traveller.
