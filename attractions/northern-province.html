Discover the Spirit of Northern Sri Lanka

Northern Province offers a distinctive travel experience shaped by Tamil culture, Hindu and Buddhist heritage, colonial history, remote islands, lagoons, beaches and traditional communities. From the vibrant cultural centre of Jaffna to the historic landscapes of Mannar and the quieter northern districts, the region provides travellers with an opportunity to explore a different side of Sri Lanka.
