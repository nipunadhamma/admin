The Cultural Heart of Sri Lanka

Central Province is a spectacular combination of ancient heritage, sacred places, dramatic mountains, tea plantations, cool highland towns and cascading waterfalls. From the historic city of Kandy to the archaeological treasures of Matale and the picturesque landscapes of Nuwara Eliya, this region offers some of Sri Lanka's most memorable travel experiences.

The spiritual and cultural heart of Sri Lanka, famous for the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, Kandy Lake, historic landmarks and mountain scenery.
