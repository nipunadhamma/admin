Discover the breathtaking landscapes of Sri Lanka's Uva Province, from the misty mountains and tea-covered hills of Ella and Badulla to the ancient heritage, waterfalls, wildlife and cultural treasures of Monaragala.

The Land of Mountains, Waterfalls and Adventure

Uva Province is one of Sri Lanka's most scenic travel regions, combining misty mountain landscapes, tea plantations, spectacular waterfalls, ancient Buddhist heritage and rich wildlife experiences. From the world-famous scenery of Ella and the historic attractions of Badulla to the cultural and natural treasures of Monaragala, Uva offers unforgettable experiences for nature lovers, hikers, photographers, history enthusiasts and adventure travellers.
