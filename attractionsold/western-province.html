Explore Western Province

Discover the vibrant heart of Sri Lanka, from the cosmopolitan energy of Colombo to the golden beaches of Negombo and the peaceful coastal landscapes of Kalutara.

The Gateway to Sri Lanka

Western Province combines Sri Lanka's commercial capital, historic landmarks, lively urban experiences, lagoons, wetlands and beautiful western coastline. Whether you are looking for culture, history, shopping, food, nature or a relaxing beach escape, the province offers something for every traveller.
