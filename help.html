FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about LankaWayfarer.

Can I explore Sri Lanka without creating an account?

Yes. You can explore destinations and use the public parts of the website without creating an account. An account is required for features such as managing your trip and quotation requests.

Why do I need to verify my email?

Email verification helps confirm ownership of your registered email address and helps protect your account.

I didn't receive my verification email. What should I do?

First check your Spam or Junk folder. If you still cannot find it, use the Resend Verification Email option on the verification page.

What is a quotation request?

A quotation request is a message containing your planned trip details that you send to a registered guide. The guide can review your requirements and prepare a personalized quotation.

Can I choose my own guide?

Yes. You can review available registered guide profiles and select a guide that matches your travel requirements.

What information can I see about a guide?

Depending on the profile, you may see information such as the guide's photo, name, district, languages, experience and verification status.

What happens after I send a quotation request?

The request is delivered to the selected guide. The guide can review your trip requirements and send a personalized quotation through the platform.

Can I use LankaWayfarer on my phone?

Yes. LankaWayfarer is designed to work across desktop, tablet and mobile screen sizes.

How do I become a registered guide?

Choose the Guide registration option, complete your profile information and follow the verification process.
